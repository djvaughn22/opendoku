How to play
  • Tap a square to place a photo tile. Tap it again to take it back.
  • Every row, every column, and every color gets exactly one tile.
  • After you place a tile, the squares it rules out get a small dot.
  • Turn on ✕ Mark to cross out squares yourself while you think.
  • If two tiles break a rule, they turn red and the message says why.
  • 💡 Hint takes off a tile that's in the wrong spot, or shows one right square.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can reach it without guessing.
  • 📤 Send makes a link with the same board and photo for family or friends.
