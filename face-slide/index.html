How to play
  • Tap a tile next to the empty square to slide it in. Tap a tile further along the same row or column to slide the whole line.
  • You can also swipe on the board, or use the arrow keys.
  • The puzzle is done when the photo is whole again. The bottom-right piece drops in at the end.
  • Hold 👀 Peek to see the finished photo. Turn on 🔢 Numbers if you'd like each tile numbered in order.
  • Every shuffle can be solved.
  • 📤 Send makes a link with the same shuffle and photo for family or friends.
