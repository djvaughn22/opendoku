How to play
  • Tap a tile next to the empty square to slide it in. Tap a tile further along the same row or column to slide the whole line at once.
  • You can also swipe on the board, or use the arrow keys.
  • Put the tiles in order: 1 in the top-left, counting across each row, with the gap ending in the bottom-right.
  • A good way in: finish the top row, then the left column, and keep shrinking the puzzle.
  • Hold 👀 Peek to see the finished picture. 🔢 Numbers turns the tile numbers on or off.
  • Every shuffle can be solved.
