How to play
  • Tap an empty square, then tap a picture below the board to fill it. Tap 🧽 to empty a square.
  • Every row, every column, and every box (the blocks with thicker lines) gets each picture exactly once.
  • Pictures that repeat in a row, column, or box turn red.
  • 💡 Hint takes out a wrong picture, or fills in a square that can only be one thing.
  • Every puzzle has one answer, and you can reach it without guessing.
  • On a keyboard: arrow keys move, number keys place pictures in palette order, Backspace empties.
